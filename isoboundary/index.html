Business benefits

Reduce endpoint trust without slowing people down.

IsoBoundary shifts the question from “can we trust this laptop?” to “what is this browser session allowed to do?”

01

Onboard third parties faster

Give contractors a browser entry point instead of extending your endpoint stack, VPN posture or full VDI footprint.

02

Contain hostile web execution

Run protected browsing remotely in disposable Firecracker microVMs rather than on the unmanaged endpoint.

03

Control what comes back

Downloads and clipboard data cross through explicit brokers, so policy applies to the boundary—not only the browser process.

04

Create stronger audit evidence

Capture session, policy, file, clipboard, release and administrative events as attributable decisions.

05

Move from full to selective isolation

Steer destinations into DIRECT, ISOLATE or BLOCK paths as deployment expands.

06

Keep infrastructure sovereignty

Target customer-controlled deployment with a deliberately small Erlang/OTP control plane.

A protected download becomes a release workflow: quarantine, validation, scanning, optional sanitization, then a short-lived authorized release.
